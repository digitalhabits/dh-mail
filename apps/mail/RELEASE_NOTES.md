## What is new

- **Exchange needs no access code.** Connect Exchange opens straight on your email address, username and password. The app talks only to your Exchange server when you connect.
- **See what a reply quotes.** Under the reply box, "Show quoted text" shows the earlier messages that go with your reply, as a forward shows the message it carries.
- **Clearer empty list.** With no mailbox connected, the list says "Connect an account to get started", with Gmail, Outlook and Exchange side by side. With every mailbox hidden, it says so, how each one is hidden, and offers "Show in Mail now".
