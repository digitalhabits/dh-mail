## What is new

**Connecting a Microsoft 365 account**
- At a university or company where IT has approved Mail for everyone, you can now connect your account. Before, Mail asked Microsoft to show a consent screen each time, and that screen stopped people who may not approve apps themselves.

**When you remove an account**
- Its senders and subjects also leave the saved message lists, and its pinned conversations are removed. Before, they stayed in the lists that draw before your mail loads.

**Other changes**
- Cmd+Plus and Cmd+Minus (Ctrl+Plus and Ctrl+Minus on Windows) change the text size in a conversation, a person's mail or a draft that is open in a window of its own.
- A Sync you press just after Mail opens is no longer cancelled.
