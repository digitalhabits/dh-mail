## What is new

**The list**
- **Threads from one person are a stack.** A person with several threads is one row, with "4 threads" by the name. Click it to see each thread under it. The newest opens, and its row is marked, so you can see which one is open. Click the row again to fold the stack.
- **How many mails.** Every thread says how many mails it holds, after the sender.
- **A clearer wide list.** When the list is wide, a row shows the subject, then the sender, with the first words of the message under them.
- **A tighter list.** Rows sit closer together, with the subject under the sender.
- **The whole window for the list.** Drag the list's edge past the reader, and let go to show the list across the whole window. While you drag, Mail tells you when letting go will do this.

**The mail list's button**
- **Always in the same place.** The button that hides and shows the mail list stays beside the window's buttons. New email moves over with the list.
- **Hover to bring the list out.** When the list is hidden, point at the button to bring it out, and click it to keep it out. The list goes away again when you move to a message.

**Reading**
- **Text size.** The Aa button sets the text size, in the thread and in the Reader.
- **The thread's heading is one line.** The subject is on the left, and the people are on the right. How many mails and the dates are in the bar above.
- **The first message.** In a long thread, click the dates to see the first message.
- **Wider messages in the whole window.** In a thread over the whole window, drag a message's edge to make it wider.

**Your mailboxes**
- **When a mailbox is shown.** Schedules now say when a mailbox is shown and when new mail arrives.
- **A new mailbox asks about your week.** When you connect a mailbox, Mail asks when you want to see it.
- **Hide again after.** A mailbox you show early can hide itself again after a time you choose.

**Other**
- The folder list is cleaner, with a quieter mark on the open folder.
- The welcome screens show where things are now.
- The dialog to show a hidden mailbox early is dark in the dark theme.
