## What is new

- **Search lands where it should.** A thread opened from a search goes to the message that matched, and stays there while the rest of the thread loads. The words you searched for are marked in that message.
- **Your signature keeps its style.** A saved signature, draft or auto-reply now opens in the editor with its own text size and colour. Before, they came back at the default size, in black.
- **"Sync paused" goes away after you reconnect.** The notice no longer stays on screen once the mailbox is syncing again.
- **Dialogs sit on top.** The Signature dialog and the other settings dialogs are drawn over the whole window, so nothing from behind shows through them.
